Shopify Product Title Guide
Formatting rules for scheduling staff when creating session products in Shopify.
The Format
time date | Session Title
All three parts are required, separated by a space-pipe-space |
Part 1 — Time
  • 12-hour format, no space before am/pm
  • Use a colon for minutes only when not on the hour
  • Times starting with 9 must have an asterisk prefix for correct sorting
✅ Correct ❌ Wrong
*9am9am
*9:30am9:30am / 9:30 am
10am10:00am / 10 am
6:30pm6:30 pm / 6pm30
Part 2 — Date
Format: Day Mon DDth
  • Day: 3-letter abbreviation — Mon Tue Wed Thu Fri Sat Sun
  • Month: 3-letter abbreviation — Jan Feb Mar Apr May Jun Jul Aug Sep Oct Nov Dec
  • Day number with suffix: 1st 2nd 3rd 4th … 21st 22nd 23rd … 29th 30th 31st
✅ Correct ❌ Wrong
Sun May 10thSunday May 10 / Sun May 10
Wed Apr 29thWed April 29th / Wednesday Apr 29th
Mon Jun 2ndMon June 2
Part 3 — Session Title
Use the standard session name exactly as it appears in the system. Keep it clean — no extra details or artwork names. Use "and" instead of "&" to avoid any sync issues.
Full Examples — Standard Sessions
3pm Sun May 10th | Wheel Workshop
*9:30am Sat Jun 7th | Kids Canvas Class
6:30pm Fri May 2nd | Wednesday Paint Night
Multi-Week Kids Sessions
Add a third segment for the series length:
time date | Session Title | X Week Session
5:45pm Mon Apr 13th | Post-Impressionists | 10 Week Session
4pm Wed Sep 9th | Lawren Harris and the Group of Seven Part 1 | 6 Week Session
ℹ️ You only create one product — the system automatically generates Class 2, Class 3, etc. for the remaining weeks.
Variants (Studio Locations)
Each product must have a variant for every studio that offers that session:
BC / VANCOUVER - MAIN STREET
ON / BURLINGTON - SOUTH
AB / CALGARY - INGLEWOOD
⚠️ Why This Matters
The dashboard reads product titles to match Shopify registrations to the correct session and studio. If the format is off — wrong separator, extra words, missing asterisk, inconsistent spacing — the sync will fail and bookings won't appear on the schedule.
