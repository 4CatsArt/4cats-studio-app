How email templates work

Every customer email's wording lives here. Change it here and the next email sent uses the new wording — no code, no deploy.

Two kinds of templates

🔒 AutomaticSent by a button, e.g. kiln's "→ Ready for Pickup" or "⏰ Send last chance email". You can change the subject and wording any time. You can't delete them or change what triggers them. A new automatic email (a new button) needs a small build.
Pick-from-a-listStaff choose these when writing an email themselves. Add, edit and delete freely.

Where a template shows up

Staff email toolThe Email button on the studio dashboard / schedule page. Staff pick it, can still edit it, then send.
Community dashboardCoordinators' marketing suggestions. These only ever go to customers who opted in to marketing. Also set Category to "Seasonal Marketing" — that's what the dashboard looks for today.
Kiln: workshop / party ticketsUsed by kiln's built-in 🔒 emails. Kiln doesn't have a "pick a template" list yet, so a new non-🔒 template ticked here won't appear in kiln.
Camp artwork pickupComing soon — not switched over yet.

Studios at the bottom of the editor limits a template to certain studios. Leave it on all studios unless it's location-specific (e.g. a closure).

Adding a new template

  1. Click + New Template.
  2. Give it a clear name — this is what staff see in their list.
  3. Tick where it can be used.
  4. Choose the Type:
    • Transactional — about something they booked or bought (reminder, closure, pickup).
    • Marketing — anything that promotes something new (sales, camps, "we miss you"). Marketing should only be sent from the community dashboard, which already sends only to opted-in customers.
  5. Write the subject and message (formatting below).
  6. Save, then test it (see the end of this page).
⚠️ Keep transactional emails purely transactional. Adding a promotion ("…and check out our new camps!") turns it into a marketing email under Canada's anti-spam law, and it then needs the customer's opt-in.

Formatting

Type thisYou get
A blank lineA new paragraph
**Saturday 2pm**Saturday 2pm
[Book now](https://4cats.com/…)A link that says "Book now"
A pasted https://… linkA clickable link. Links get ?studio=… added automatically so we can see which studio sent the click.
Kiln templates only:
# Your piece is ready! on its own lineA big heading
[[Book glazing|https://…]] on its own lineA big red button
{{ticket_box}} on its own lineThe grey "Your Ticket" box with the ticket number

Layout (kiln templates): Branded = logo header and grey footer. Plain = looks like a personal note. Every kiln email gets the studio name, address and hello@4cats.com at the bottom automatically.

Fill-in tokens

Type these exactly, with the double curly brackets. They're replaced for each person. The editor lists the ones that work for the places you've ticked.

{{first_name}}Customer's first name ("there" if we don't have one)
{{studio_name}}The studio's name
{{calendar_link}}A link to the studio's Instagram page (the pinned post has opening times). Studios with no Instagram get their calendar page instead. Reads well as "check our {{calendar_link}} for opening times".
{{class_name}}, {{date}}Staff email tool: the class name, and today's date
{{ticket_number}}Kiln: the ticket number
{{session_title}}Kiln: the workshop or party name
{{description}}Kiln workshop tickets: the note on the ticket
[our pinned post here]({{instagram_url}})Kiln: a link to the studio's Instagram page with your own wording. Studios without Instagram get their calendar page instead.
{{instagram_link}}Kiln: the studio's Instagram handle as a link, e.g. @4cats_erin

A misspelled token shows up in the email as-is (e.g. {{frist_name}}) — so read your test email carefully.

Testing before studios use it

  • Kiln emails: use the test party ticket PT-TEST-1 (emails renee@4cats.com), or put your own email on a workshop ticket's sign-in.
  • Staff tool: pick the template on a class you're booked into, or read it through carefully — every send also copies hello@4cats.com.
  • Check: tokens filled in, links work, nothing promotional in a transactional email.

Need something new?

Ask for a build when you want: a new automatic email (a new button or trigger), a new token, a new place a template can be used, or a kiln "pick a template" list.

Shopify Product Title Guide
Formatting rules for scheduling staff when creating session products in Shopify.
The Format
time date | Session Title
All three parts are required, separated by a space-pipe-space |
Part 1 — Time
  • 12-hour format, no space before am/pm
  • Use a colon for minutes only when not on the hour
  • Times starting with 9 must have an asterisk prefix for correct sorting
✅ Correct ❌ Wrong
*9am9am
*9:30am9:30am / 9:30 am
10am10:00am / 10 am
6:30pm6:30 pm / 6pm30
Part 2 — Date
Format: Day Mon DDth
  • Day: 3-letter abbreviation — Mon Tue Wed Thu Fri Sat Sun
  • Month: 3-letter abbreviation — Jan Feb Mar Apr May Jun Jul Aug Sep Oct Nov Dec
  • Day number with suffix: 1st 2nd 3rd 4th … 21st 22nd 23rd … 29th 30th 31st
✅ Correct ❌ Wrong
Sun May 10thSunday May 10 / Sun May 10
Wed Apr 29thWed April 29th / Wednesday Apr 29th
Mon Jun 2ndMon June 2
Part 3 — Session Title
Use the standard session name exactly as it appears in the system. Keep it clean — no extra details or artwork names. Use "and" instead of "&" to avoid any sync issues.
Full Examples — Standard Sessions
3pm Sun May 10th | Wheel Workshop
*9:30am Sat Jun 7th | Kids Canvas Class
6:30pm Fri May 2nd | Wednesday Paint Night
Multi-Week Kids Sessions
Add a third segment for the series length:
time date | Session Title | X Week Session
5:45pm Mon Apr 13th | Post-Impressionists | 10 Week Session
4pm Wed Sep 9th | Lawren Harris and the Group of Seven Part 1 | 6 Week Session
ℹ️ You only create one product — the system automatically generates Class 2, Class 3, etc. for the remaining weeks.
Variants (Studio Locations)
Each product must have a variant for every studio that offers that session:
BC / VANCOUVER - MAIN STREET
ON / BURLINGTON - SOUTH
AB / CALGARY - INGLEWOOD
⚠️ Why This Matters
The dashboard reads product titles to match Shopify registrations to the correct session and studio. If the format is off — wrong separator, extra words, missing asterisk, inconsistent spacing — the sync will fail and bookings won't appear on the schedule.

📈 Fill Rates

How full each studio gets, by seats in its floor plan. The big number is day occupancy — the share of seat-time used across the open hours (busier = greener). The small number is the busiest moment that day; ⚠ means it hit 85%+ of seats, so the studio came close to running out of room. Only bookings take seats — a closed class never does. Open hours come from what was offered that day. Click a cell to see that day's timeline.

Day occupancy: under 10% 10–19% 20–34% 35–49% 50%+ ⚠ busiest moment 85%+ of seats

🎨 Friday nights

Friday fill for every studio — every past Friday, then the next 8 (booked so far, dashed). Seat-time from 5:30 to 9:30 pm from every booking and drop-in, against a realistic full night — the same number as the coordinators' Friday card and Friday points. Colours follow the points tiers. Click a column to sort; hover a cell for guests and the event's share.

under 20% 20–39% 40%+ (tier 1) 50%+ (tier 2) 80%+ (tier 3) upcoming — booked so far

🏆 Community Points

Set what coordinators earn, manage prizes and levels, and handle redemptions. Friday fill points are awarded every Saturday morning; party fill and team building on the 2nd of each month. Editing a rule never changes points already awarded.
