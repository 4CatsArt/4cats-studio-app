Builds shifts for one studio and week from what the studio needs (Coverage), who's available, home studios, badges, weekly hours and overtime. Approved shifts already in the week are kept and only the gaps are filled. With Autosave on, changes are saved as drafts a moment after each edit; otherwise click Save as drafts. Saving replaces this studio's earlier drafts for the week. Unfilled shifts aren't saved. Hover ⓘ next to a name for their hours, home studio, badges and notes. Drag a shift onto another person or day to move it, or onto Unfilled shifts to take it off someone. Click a shift's time to change it (e.g. 8:30a-5p). Add up to 4 studios to see and build them together: someone planned at one won't be double-booked at another.
